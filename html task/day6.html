CHEESE PASTA RECIPE

LIST OF THINGS

  • Pasta
  • Maida
  • Milk
  • Cheese
  • Garlic
  • Butter
  • Oregano
  • chilly flakes
  • salt & pepper

STEP BY STEP RECIPE MAKING

  1. Boil water and cook pasta until al dente.
  2. Heat butter in a pan.
  3. Sauté garlic until fragrant.
  4. Add maida flour mix into the pan.
  5. Mix it untill raw smell went,then add milk.
  6. Let them cook untill creame consistancy.
  7. Add cooked pasta into the pan and mix well add sone cheese, salt,pepper and seasoning flavours.
