Understanding Semantic HTML

Why Semantic Tags Matter

Semantic HTML tags like <header>, <article>, and <footer> give meaning to your content, improving accessibility and SEO.

Examples of Semantic Tags

<article>

Used for self-contained content such as blog posts, news stories, or forum entries.

<aside>

Used for supplementary content like sidebars, related links, or advertisements.
